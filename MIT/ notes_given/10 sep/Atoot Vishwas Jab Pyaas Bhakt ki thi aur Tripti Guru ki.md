**Atoot Vishwas: Jab Pyaas Bhakt ki thi aur Tripti Guru ki**  
Satsang ki roohani urja se labrez hokar Guruji laut rahe the. Raste mein unhone achanak driver se kaha, "Bhaiya, kahin chai pilwa do."  
Driver ne rasookh dekha toh ek aalishan 5-star hotel ke samne break maar diya. Guruji muskuraye aur bole, "Nahi, yahan nahi, aur aage chalo." Driver ne phir ek nami restaurant par gaadi roki, par Guruji ka mann wahan bhi nahi paseeja. Chalte-chalte koson door ek dhool bhari sadak par ek mamooli sa dhaba aaya. Guruji ki aankhon mein chamak aayi, bole— "Bas, yahin ruk jao!"  
Hairan driver dukan par pahuncha aur kadak chai ka order diya. Jaise hi dukandaar ne paise rakhne ke liye apna galla khola, driver ki nazarein thithak gayin. Wahan Guruji ka ek sundar swaroop (photo) laga tha.  
"Tum inhein jante ho?" driver ne poochha.  
Dukandaar ki aankhon mein ek tees ubhari, woh bola— "Sahab, inse milne ke liye pai-pai jodi thi, par pichhle hafte woh chori ho gayi. Jaa nahi saka... par mera dil kehta hai, agar mera pyaar saccha hai, toh Guruji khud yahan milne aayenge."  
Driver ka seena shraddha se bhar aaya. Usne chai ka pyala wapas rakha aur kaha— "Bhai, aaj teri muraad poori hui. Jaa, yeh chai khud uss car mein dekar aa."  
Dukandaar hichkichaya, "Sahab, agar main gaya toh kahin bache-khuche paise phir chori na ho jayein?" Driver ne bharosa diya, "Tu jaa, tere nuksaan ki bharpai main apni jeb se karunga."  
Jaise hi woh dukandaar chai lekar car ki khidki tak pahuncha, uske haath kampne lage. Samne wahi chehra tha, wahi muskaan thi jise woh roz tasveer mein niharta tha. Uski aankhon se aansuon ka sailaab beh nikla.  
Guruji ne pyaar se uska haath thama aur bole— "Pagli aatma, tune hi toh kaha tha ki main tujhse yahin milne aaoon. Ab jab main aa gaya hoon, toh tu ro raha hai?"  
**Seekh:** Itihaas gawah hai, bhagwan ko paane ke liye makhmali kaaleenon ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Jab mann mein sacchai aur iradon mein pakeezgi ho, toh kayanat ko bhi rasta badalna padta hai. Shraddha mein woh chumbak hai jo sakshat Ishwar ko aapke dwar par kheench laati hai.

**Unbreakable Faith: When the Devotee Had the Thirst and the Guru Had the Fulfillment**  
Filled with the spiritual energy of the *Satsang* (spiritual discourse), Guruji was on his way back. Along the way, he suddenly said to his driver, "Brother, let's stop somewhere for some tea."  
Considering Guruji's stature, the driver hit the brakes in front of a luxurious 5-star hotel. Guruji smiled and said, "No, not here, keep going." The driver then stopped at a renowned restaurant, but Guruji remained unmoved. After traveling for miles, they came across an ordinary *dhaba* (roadside eatery) on a dusty road. A spark lit up in Guruji's eyes, and he said, "Stop right here!"

The surprised driver walked up to the shop and ordered a strong cup of tea. As soon as the shopkeeper opened his cash drawer to put the money inside, the driver's gaze froze. Inside the drawer rested a beautiful photograph of Guruji.  
"Do you know him?" the driver asked.  
A pang of sorrow appeared in the shopkeeper's eyes. He replied, "Sir, I had saved every single penny to go meet him, but last week my savings were stolen. I couldn't go... but my heart tells me that if my devotion is true, Guruji himself will come here to meet me."  
The driver's heart swelled with reverence. He set the cup of tea back down on the counter and said, "Brother, today your wish has been granted. Go, serve this tea to the person in that car yourself."  
The shopkeeper hesitated. "Sir, if I leave the counter, what if my remaining money gets stolen too?" The driver reassured him, "You go. If you lose anything, I will compensate you from my own pocket."  
As the shopkeeper approached the car window holding the tea, his hands began to tremble. Right in front of him was the very same face, the very same smile he gazed at every day in his photograph. A flood of tears poured from his eyes.  
Guruji lovingly held his hand and said, "Silly soul, you were the one who asked me to come meet you right here. Now that I am here, why are you crying?"  
**The Lesson:**  
History bears witness that you do not need velvet carpets to reach God. When there is truth in your heart and purity in your intentions, even the universe shifts its course to make a way. True faith is the magnet that pulls the Divine right to your doorstep.

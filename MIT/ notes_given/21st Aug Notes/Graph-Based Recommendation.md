**Graph-Based Recommendation**  
basic..  
  
**Definition:** Use user-item interaction graphs or knowledge graphs for recommendations. wordnet..  
 **Examples:**  
**User-Item Bipartite Graphs:**  
**Example:** Facebook recommends friends based on mutual friend connections (a form of graph-based collaborative filtering).  
**Knowledge Graph Integration:**  
**Example:** Alibaba’s recommender integrates a product knowledge graph to provide **semantically rich recommendations** across domains (e.g., dresses linked with accessories or styles).
